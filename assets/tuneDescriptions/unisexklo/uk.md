Inspired by the German song "Unisexklo" (All-gender toilet) from the rapper
Ikkimel ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ikkimel)) and introduced by
members of RoR Würzburg at TNM 2026. Very energetic tune, although tricky to
play.

**Sign:** Form a fist with one hand and hold the palm of your other hand next to
it looks like a toilet seat. Close and open the lid.

## Background

**Name:** "Unisexklo" translates to "all-gender toilet"
([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unisex_public_toilet)) and is also
the title of the song by Ikkimel, which served as the musical inspiration. In
general, the authors consider all-gender or gender-inclusive toilets a positive
thing.

**Musical background:** Based on the song "Unisexklo" by the German rapper
Ikkimel ([Youtube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BUWdx7m8id8)). The Surdos
resemble the bass line of the song, while the agogô resembles a rhythmic voice
line. Repinique and Tamborim imitate the rapper's voice in the refrain.

The tofu kickback break is inspired by the song "Eisgekühlter Bommerlunder"
([Youtube](https://youtu.be/t03Nh0hyBY4?si=8gmdDacvrFT61yjC)) by the German Punk
Rock Band "Die toten Hosen"
([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eisgek%C3%BChlter_Bommerlunder)),
which in turn was inspired by the Samba-song "Chiu Chiu"
([Youtube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HbREwS9bvO8)) by the Chilean composer
Nicanor Molinare Rencoret. It is repeated over and over, getting faster
continuously.

**History:** A member of RoR Würzburg was listening to the Unisexklo song and
noticed it could sound very nice with the RoR instruments. Together with another
band member they created this tune, spread it in their local group and
introduced it spontaneously at the TNM 2026 in Austria.

**Political controversy:** The RoRies who adapted the tune are aware of the
ongoing controversy about Ikkimel and her texts. The rapper appropriates the
violent and sexualizing style of some male rappers and turns it around to
degrade and sexualize men, thus reproducing violent language. As an example, she
reclaims misogynistic terms like "slut" or "cunt" to describe herself. Some see
a powerful self-determined feminist position in her behaviour, some criticize
her for reproducing violent language. The glorification of drug abuse and the
ableist language she uses are definitely problematic. At the same time she
clearly is not serious about it and uses irony a lot. In the song "Unisexklo"
she raps (translated) "Aha, oho, meet me at the all-gender toilet. Shove your
university up your ass. Dumb fucks well, I am dumb as a doorknob", promoting
herself as dumb and therefore a good fucker while actually having a bachelor's
degree in linguistics. For this RoR tune, this line of the refrain was
translated into the english memory hook "I fuck well and I need to pee" in order
not to reproduce ableist language, as pointed out at the TNM 2026. The RoR
Würzburg members who introduced the tune especially like the cheeky sex-positive
vibe of the original song.

## Difficulty

Except for Agogô and Snare, all instruments are quite tricky and require some
practice.

* **Surdos:** medium
* **Repi:** hard
* **Snare:** easy
* **Tamborim:** hard
* **Agogô:** easy

## Breaks

* Intro: only Surdos and Agogô play for four bars
* Break 1: Everybody singing "A-Ha, O-Ho". What is special about this break is
  that if the main groove follows after break 1, it is continued from the second
  bar on (since the A-Ha O-Ho singing is already included in the first bar).
  When break 1 is repeated in a loop or the 4x crescendo it is easier to start
  the groove on the first bar afterwards.
* Break 2: Everybody plays the Tamborim part with some accents. The memory hook
  is either the original refrain text or "A-Ha, O-Ho, meet me at the **Uni**
  sexklo. Fuck you and your **Uni** degree, I fuck **well** and I need to pee".
  All drums play the accents on the rim and Agogô plays high. The accents are
  timed on **uni** and **well**.
* Tofu kick back: repeats until cut. It's fun to start slowly and keep getting
  faster during the loop. The memory hook is "Here, a slice of bread with tofu.
  TOFU! Here, a slice of bread with corn. CORN! Now, we have these two bread
  slices, HEY! One with tofu, one with corn. Next to that..."
